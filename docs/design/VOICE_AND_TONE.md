# Bevoya voice and tone

Version 1 · 20 October 2026
Sources: brand guide (section 5), design file (section 14), master prompt (notifications), Brand Identity Board (taglines).

## 1. Voice

Bevoya sounds **supportive, clear, respectful, practical, evidence-aware, encouraging and honest.** It is personal but respects privacy. It motivates without pressure. Sentence case everywhere. Plain words, short sentences. No jargon. No exclamation-mark overload.

Lines to reuse (from your files and the board):

- Tagline: **Your life, moving forward.**
- Supporting lines: "Big goals. Small steps. Real progress." and "Your journey. Your pace. Our support."
- Other taglines to keep in reserve: "Become, one day at a time." and "Make progress personal."

## 2. Say this, not that

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "You made progress on three sessions this week." | "You failed your streak." |
| "Your notes suggest practice questions may help." | "You are falling behind." |
| "Would you like to spend 20 minutes on that today?" | "You are lazy." / "You must be productive." |
| "Here is one useful next step." | "Do this or you will fail." |
| "You can adjust this plan." | "You are not good enough." |
| "Want to pick one meaningful task?" | Fake urgency: "Last chance!", countdowns, "Don't lose your streak!" |

Rules: never guilt, shame, compare users to others, or promise results. Never guarantee grades, jobs, followers, income or success. Offer, do not command. Always leave the user in control.

## 3. Copy by situation

| Situation | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Empty state | What is missing, then one action | "No learning evidence yet. Start a session, add a note, or complete a quiz to begin building proof of progress." |
| Loading AI | What is happening, in plain words | "Reviewing your recent sessions, notes, and goal…" |
| AI unavailable | Say so, say what still works | "AI is unavailable right now. You can still plan and write as usual." |
| AI quota reached | Neutral, say when it resets | "You've used today's AI suggestions. They reset tomorrow." |
| Error | What happened, what to try | "We couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again." |
| Offline | Reassure | "You're offline. Your changes are saved on this device and will sync later." |
| Missed days | Neutral, forward-looking | "Welcome back. Want to pick one small step for today?" |
| Permission request | Why first, then ask, with an out | "Bevoya can show focus patterns only if you allow usage access. Your data stays private, and you can turn this off any time." Buttons: Allow, Not now |
| Free-plan limit | Facts, no pressure | "The free plan includes one active goal. Pro adds unlimited goals." |
| Deletion | Clear, named | "Delete this reflection? This cannot be undone." Buttons: Delete reflection, Keep it |
| Success | Short, calm | "Saved." |

## 4. AI copy

- Always label AI content "AI insight".
- Say why: "Based on your recent sessions and quiz results…".
- Separate facts from the user's data and AI interpretation. Show uncertainty: "I'm not sure, because there's no mood recorded."
- Footer: "AI suggestions are guidance, not professional advice."
- Never present a recommendation as a decision. Use "Would you like to…" and offer Accept, Edit, Dismiss.

## 5. Notifications

- Ask before enabling. Let the user choose types. Respect quiet hours.
- Calm and short. Example: "You have 45 minutes available today. Want to choose one meaningful task?"
- **No private content** in a notification: no journal text, goal names, or reflection excerpts. Use generic prompts.
- No guilt, no streak threats, no fake urgency, no "we miss you" pressure.

## 6. Sensitive moments

Bevoya is not therapy and does not diagnose. If a user repeatedly writes about exhaustion, distress or harmful patterns, respond with care: acknowledge, encourage rest and talking to someone they trust, and suggest professional or local help where appropriate. Do not label a condition. Do not minimise. Have a qualified person review this copy before beta.

## 7. Style rules

1. Sentence case for buttons, titles and labels.
2. Buttons are verbs: "Start session", "Save reflection", "Add task".
3. One idea per sentence, under about 20 words where possible.
4. Numbers: "3 sessions", "25 minutes".
5. Emoji: none in system messages. Rare, sparing use in celebration moments only (proposal).
6. Dates and times in the user's locale and time zone.
7. Write for the Senior-friendly mode by default: plain, clear, no idiom.
