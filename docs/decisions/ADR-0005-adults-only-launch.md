# ADR-0005: Launch for adults (18+)

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 20 October 2026
**Deciders:** Founder (Leo Noel Zuze AkA jayzthe_plug)

## Context
The files disagree: the master prompt says 13+ or 18+; the project brief says 18+. India's DPDP Act requires verifiable parental consent for children, and Google Play's families policies apply if children are in the audience.

## Decision
Launch for ages 18 and over. Record a self-declared 18+ confirmation in consent records at onboarding. Design a teen mode later, after legal review.

## Options Considered

### Option A: 18+ only

Simplest legal position; the product's reflection and data features suit adults first.

### Option B: 13+ with teen mode

Larger audience, but needs parental consent flows, stricter data handling and legal review before launch.

## Consequences
- Easier: fewer legal obligations at launch.
- Harder: students under 18 cannot use the app.
- Revisit: after legal review and a designed teen mode.
